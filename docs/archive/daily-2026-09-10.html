💵 Real Yield / Rate Linkage

Higher US nominal yields driven by Fed hike bets push US real yields (nominal rates minus inflation expectations) higher. This increases the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding bullion—typically a strong bearish signal.

Simultaneously, BOJ tightening threatens a carry-trade unwind. While higher US real yields act as a direct cap on gold, BOJ rate hikes narrow the policy gap and increase market volatility. In this setup, the "higher real yield" channel is currently dominating price action, keeping gold suppressed. However, the latent threat of a disorderly carry-trade unwind creates a resilient floor via safe-haven hedging.
