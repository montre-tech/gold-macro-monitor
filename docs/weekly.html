💵 Real Yield / Rate Linkage

US real yields remain the primary anchor for gold. A wide Fed-BOJ policy rate differential continues to incentivize borrowing cheap yen to fund higher-yielding dollar assets. This widening differential supports USD strength, directly weighing on gold via firm real yields.

When evaluating BOJ headlines or potential intervention (MOF/BOJ buying yen), distinguish between two competing channels:

1. **The Real Yield Channel:** Broad Fed yield dominance keeps opportunity costs high for holding gold.

2. **The Carry-Trade Unwind Channel:** Intervention itself does not mechanically shift US Treasury yields; it signals policy discomfort. If BOJ policy tightening or sudden market panic triggers an aggressive USD/JPY carry unwind, the resulting risk-off shock can spark heavy safe-haven flows *into* gold, overriding the higher real yield channel.

Currently, with no active carry unwind underway, the elevated real yield channel remains dominant.
