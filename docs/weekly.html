💵 Real Yield / Rate Linkage

US real yields (nominal Treasury yields minus inflation expectations) remain gold's primary fundamental driver. A widening Fed-BOJ policy rate differential incentivizes the USD/JPY carry trade, supporting dollar strength and lifting US real yields—creating a fundamental drag on gold.

When BOJ policy or intervention headlines emerge, two competing channels must be explicitly weighed:

  • **Real Yield Channel:** High/sticky US real yields make non-yielding gold less attractive.
  • **Unwind Channel:** A sharp narrowing of the rate differential or heavy yen buying signals central bank discomfort, raising carry-trade unwind risk. An unwind is a volatile, risk-off event that drives safe-haven flows *into* gold, often overriding the real-yield drag. Note that MOF/BOJ FX intervention signals rate path discomfort rather than mechanically moving US Treasury yields directly.

Right now, absent an active carry-unwind panic, sticky US real yields remain a steady headwind, but underlying structural demand is cushioning the downside.
